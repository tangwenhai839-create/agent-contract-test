# Code of Conduct

We are committed to a welcoming, harassment-free project for everyone.

Be respectful, assume good intent while verifying technical claims, give actionable feedback, and avoid personal attacks or discriminatory language. Maintainers may edit or remove abusive content and restrict participation when necessary to protect the community.

Report conduct concerns privately to the maintainers through the repository's security or contact channel. Reports will be handled as confidentially as practical.
